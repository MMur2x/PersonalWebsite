01 · Purpose

What the portfolio covers

I'm a Computer Science student in the UMass Lowell Honors College, graduating May 2027. My interests include backend development, infrastructure, automation, systems integration, and tools that make complex systems easier to understand.

The portfolio records three internships, four personal projects, the technologies I've used, and the communities I build with. The experience pages keep the companies, roles, dates, and measured results alongside the technical details. The project pages show what each tool does and how it was built.

For a quick introduction, start with About. To review the work directly, start with Experience or Projects.

03 · Reading modes

Choose how to read Experience

The three controls on Experience provide optional ways to review the professional record. Their names describe reading concepts here:

  • Clarity — notice the numbers. Reveals the impact readouts on internship cards so the measured results are easy to scan. Select it again to hide those readouts.
  • Insight — look deeper. Opens all internship details together, including the technical bullets and technology lists. Select it again to close them. Each card also has its own detail button.
  • Stillness — pause and review. Opens highlights from experience, projects, leadership, and education, with links to the full records, résumé, email, and LinkedIn.

On Experience, the keyboard shortcuts O, A, and C select these controls in that order. The buttons also work with a mouse, touch, or keyboard. Close the highlights with its close button or Escape.

04 · Projects

Explore the projects

  • Homework Tracker Extension brings Canvas assignments into a calendar with deadline status, daily details, notes, announcements, class views, and an agenda.
  • Cryptocurrency Investment App is a Flutter app with Dashboard, Invest, News, Learn, and Settings screens. Settings persist across restarts. Some values in the screenshots are placeholders.
  • Personal Portfolio Website is this site, built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript and deployed on GitHub Pages.
  • QR Code Generator is a Python tool that turns links and images into custom QR codes for sharing resources.

Select the detail button on any project to review its implementation and technology list. The Homework Tracker and cryptocurrency app also have screenshot galleries.

Use a gallery's previous and next buttons or its indicators to choose a screenshot. With the gallery focused, left and right arrow keys move between screens; a swipe works on touchscreens. The caption explains the selected image, and the gallery stays on that screen until you choose another.

05 · Skills

Connect skills to work

The Skills page has three groups: Languages, Frameworks & libraries, and Cloud & infrastructure. Each badge identifies a technology and where I've used it.

Hover, tap, or focus a badge to read its description. Linked badges open the relevant internship or project. Coursework badges explain the context without adding a project link. Technology chips inside work details link back to the matching skill badge when that technology appears on the Skills page.

For example, TypeScript and Kubernetes connect to the Fidelity experience, while Python connects to the QR Code Generator.

06 · Résumé and contact

Read the résumé or start a conversation

The Résumé page embeds the PDF and offers Download PDF and Open in a new tab links. If your browser does not display the embedded document, use either of those links.

The contact section includes email, LinkedIn, GitHub, and phone. Email and phone links open your usual apps. Copy buttons copy the displayed email address or phone number and confirm the result. The contact scroll opens a short invitation.

For recruiting, collaboration, or a conversation about observability tooling, email me at maynemurray@gmail.com. You can also visit LinkedIn or GitHub.

07 · Accessibility

Keyboard, themes, and motion

Use the Skip to content link to move past the header. Tab moves through links and buttons; Enter follows a link or activates a focused button, and Space activates a focused button. Detail buttons report whether their content is open.

The sun or moon button switches between light and dark themes. Your selection is remembered in your browser. System reduced-motion preferences limit decorative animations and transitions while keeping the content and controls available.

Screenshot images have text descriptions and captions. The frequently asked questions below use standard expandable disclosures: select a question to open or close its answer.

08 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where should I start?

Start with Experience for internship work and measured results, Projects for personal builds, or Résumé for the one-page overview.

Do I need to use the Experience reading modes?

The modes are optional. The measured-results section is visible on Experience, and each internship keeps its own detail button. The highlights dialog provides a quicker route through the same portfolio.

How can I see the technology behind a project?

Open that project's details to read its implementation notes and technology list. Linked technology chips lead to the matching skill badge.

Why are some skill badges linked and others not?

A linked badge points to an internship or project in this portfolio. Some skills were used in coursework, so their badges show that context without linking to a separate work record.

What if the résumé preview does not load?

Use Open in a new tab or download the PDF.

How do I get in touch?

Email Mayne, or visit the contact section for LinkedIn, GitHub, and phone.
